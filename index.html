    The honest part

    What you should know before booking

    It's a quiet neighborhood, and the county means it

    Quiet hours 10 pm – 8 am, hot tub closes at 10 pm, no events, up to 4 cars strictly off-street. These are local vacation-rental laws, not house whims — fines are real, so we say it plainly.

    No AC — like most Tahoe homes

    July–August afternoons can reach the 80s inside; fans are provided and mountain nights cool fast. Winters: heat, fireplace, and the sauna.

    Mosquitos in summer, bears all year

    The canal brings mosquitos on summer evenings — repellent is under the kitchen sink. And never leave food in the car: Tahoe bears open cars like lunchboxes.

    Winter driving is real driving

    Chains are required by law in winter storms, and snow removal can lag a heavy night. Bring boots; the reward is Heavenly, 10 minutes away.
